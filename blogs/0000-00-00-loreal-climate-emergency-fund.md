L'Oreal committed an additional 20 million euros through 2030 to its Climate Emergency Fund supporting climate-vulnerable communities, which has backed over 30 projects across 32 countries since 2023, including heat insurance for informal workers in India and AI flood prediction in Peru and Ecuador.
