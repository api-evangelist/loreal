L'Oreal reported sales of 22.47 billion euros for the first half of 2025, representing 3.0% like-for-like growth, with operating margin expanding to 21.1%. The company acquired premium skincare brand Medik8 and professional haircare brand Color Wow to strengthen its portfolio.
